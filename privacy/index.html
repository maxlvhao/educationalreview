Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Educational Review is committed to respecting your privacy. This site is designed to be read, not to collect data. We keep our practices simple and transparent.

Information We Collect

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. We do not collect personal information through the site itself.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, browser type, pages visited) as part of normal web infrastructure. This data is managed by Cloudflare under their own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to serve typefaces. When you load a page, your browser contacts Google's servers to retrieve font files. Google's privacy policy governs that interaction.

We do not use advertising networks, social media trackers, or any other third-party data collection services.

Email Contact

If you contact us by email, we will use your information only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add contacts to mailing lists or share email addresses with third parties.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to maxlu@fas.harvard.edu.
