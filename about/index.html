About

Educational Review is an independent publication dedicated to long-form writing on education, universities, and the cultivation of human potential.

Mission

We publish essays, reflections, and conversations that examine the enduring questions of education: What is a university for? How should institutions be led? What does it mean to cultivate curiosity, creativity, and courage in learners?

Each piece is given its own companion website—designed for contemplative, repeated reading rather than quick consumption. We believe that the most important ideas in education deserve the same care in presentation that they demand in thought.

The collection is open and growing. We welcome contributions from scholars, university leaders, educators, and thoughtful observers of higher education worldwide.

Editorial Standards

  • Every piece is reviewed for intellectual rigor, clarity, and originality before publication
  • We prioritize depth over volume—fewer pieces, each given full attention
  • Authors retain ownership of their work; we publish with their explicit permission
  • We do not accept paid placements, sponsored content, or advertising
  • All claims of fact are expected to be verifiable; opinions are clearly attributed
  • We value diverse perspectives across geographies, traditions, and institutional contexts
